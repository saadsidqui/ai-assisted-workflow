# P-03 · Planning, delivery and reporting

- **Where it sits:** Product and experience track
- **Who:** Required: PM / PO · Recommended: analysts · Optional: designers
- **Delivery:** Product and experience track, session 2; also usable self-serve
- **Time:** 20 min

Coordination work is mostly text: agendas, notes, status, risks. It is the fastest place to gain time and the easiest place to circulate a decision nobody made.

## Outcomes

A participant can:

- Run a meeting workflow from agenda to distributed actions with a validation step.
- Draft status reports from approved sources and check the numbers.
- Treat AI-generated plans and estimates as hypotheses.

## Topics

### Required

- Meeting flow: agenda, notes (with consent), summary, decisions validated with the attendees, actions distributed.
- Status reports and stakeholder updates are drafted from approved sources, and every number is checked.
- Estimates and plans from an assistant are hypotheses. The team estimates.
- Risk and dependency lists are prompts for discussion until reviewed.

### Recommended

- Options analysis with assumptions written out.
- Retrospective synthesis.
- Support for backlog refinement.
- One update, several versions for different stakeholders.
- Assistants connected to the tracker, read-only first.

### Optional

- Roadmap scenarios.
- Portfolio views.
- Automated status synthesis.
- Process mapping.

## Checkpoint

> The summary says "the team decided to postpone the release". Nobody remembers deciding that. What step was skipped?

## Practice

From fictional meeting notes, produce decisions and actions, then mark each as confirmed or to be confirmed.
