# B-04 · Business development

- **Where it sits:** Business and people track
- **Who:** Required: business development · Recommended: management · Optional: HR, others
- **Delivery:** Business and people track, session 2; also usable self-serve
- **Time:** 20 min

Proposals reward speed, and a single invented reference can cost the deal and the reputation. This module is about speed with nothing fabricated.

## Outcomes

A participant can:

- Prepare for a client conversation with verified research.
- Draft a proposal from approved content without invented claims.
- Keep one client's material away from another's.

## Topics

### Required

- Account and prospect research from public sources with citations, verified before it is used in a conversation.
- Discovery preparation: questions, hypotheses, stakeholder map. Follow-ups written from your own notes.
- Proposal and tender responses drafted from the approved content library, with a compliance matrix built from the request text.
- Never invented: references, case studies, certifications, figures, profile or CV content.
- Client documents and pricing stay in approved tools. One client's material is never used for another.
- Describe AI-assisted delivery to clients accurately, without over-promising ([X-01](../../overlays/X-01-working-in-a-client-environment.md), [X-03](../../overlays/X-03-end-to-end-engagement-map.md)).

### Recommended

- Mapping capabilities to stated needs.
- Tailoring profiles truthfully.
- Synthesis of win and loss notes.
- Pricing and estimation scenarios as hypotheses.
- Keeping the CRM clean with an assistant.

### Optional

- A proposal content library with retrieval.
- Agents connected to the CRM.
- Market monitoring.

## Checkpoint

> The draft proposal mentions "a similar project for a major retailer". Where did that sentence come from?

## Practice

From a fictional request for proposals, build a compliance matrix and a response outline, then mark every claim that needs a source.
