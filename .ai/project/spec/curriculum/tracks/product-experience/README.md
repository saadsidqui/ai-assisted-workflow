# Product and experience track

For product managers, product owners, analysts and designers. The track follows the path from raw discovery material to a specification that engineering, QA and a coding agent can all execute, then covers delivery coordination, design and content.

All eight core modules are required for everyone and come first: see the [curriculum index](../../README.md).

## Modules by role

Required means the role should not work with AI on this kind of task without it. Recommended means most people in the role benefit. Optional is for interest or a specific need.

## Suggested sessions

Each session is planned inside its booking with at least ten minutes left unallocated for questions and a slower pace. Module minutes come from the module cards and include each module's checkpoint.

### Session 1 · From discovery to an executable spec

- Modules: [P-01](P-01-discovery-and-synthesis.md), [P-02](P-02-requirements-and-specs.md) (45 min)
- Overlay: [X-01](../../overlays/X-01-working-in-a-client-environment.md) (10 min)
- Practice: Workshop: from a fictional brief to stories with acceptance criteria ([S-02](../../session/S-02-workshop.md)). (25 min)
- Planned: 80 min of a 90-minute booking, with 10 min unallocated

### Session 2 · Delivery and design

- Modules: [P-03](P-03-planning-delivery-and-reporting.md), [P-04](P-04-design-and-prototyping.md) (45 min)
- Overlay: [X-03](../../overlays/X-03-end-to-end-engagement-map.md) (10 min)
- Practice: Exercise: three design directions with a five-line rationale, or decisions and actions from fictional meeting notes. (15 min)
- Planned: 70 min of a 90-minute booking, with 20 min unallocated

### Session 3 · Content and data

- Modules: [P-05](P-05-content-and-ux-writing.md), [P-06](P-06-data-and-analysis.md) (40 min)
- Practice: Exercise: edit a generated paragraph, and find the planted anomaly in a small dataset. (20 min)
- Planned: 60 min of a 90-minute booking, with 30 min unallocated
