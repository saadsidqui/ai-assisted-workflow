# P-06 · Data and analysis

- **Where it sits:** Product and experience track
- **Who:** Required: analysts · Recommended: PM / PO · Optional: designers, management, business development
- **Delivery:** Product and experience track, session 3; also usable self-serve
- **Time:** 20 min

Assistants can now run real calculations on real files. They can also describe a trend that is not in the data, in a confident sentence. The difference is whether the numbers were computed or guessed.

## Outcomes

A participant can:

- Get an assistant to compute instead of estimating.
- Verify the key figures in an AI-assisted analysis.
- Decide what data may be uploaded.

## Topics

### Required

- With files and spreadsheets, have the assistant run code or formulas and show its method. Do not accept numbers it read off by eye.
- Verify: recompute the key figures, check row counts, units and filters, and check that each chart matches its data.
- Decide on data sensitivity before uploading. Aggregate or anonymize.
- State the question and the decision it supports before asking for analysis.

### Recommended

- Drafting queries with the schema as context.
- Data cleaning with a log of every change.
- Having an unfamiliar spreadsheet or model explained.
- Dashboard drafts.
- Written assumptions next to every result.

### Optional

- Assistants connected to the warehouse or BI tool.
- Forecasting and its caveats.
- Reproducible notebooks.

## Checkpoint

> The assistant reports a 12 percent increase. What are the first two things you check?

## Practice

Analyze a small fictional dataset with one planted anomaly (duplicated rows). See whether the first answer catches it.
