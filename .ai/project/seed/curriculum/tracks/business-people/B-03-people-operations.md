# B-03 · People operations

- **Where it sits:** Business and people track
- **Who:** Required: HR · Recommended: management · Optional: business development, others
- **Delivery:** Business and people track, session 2; also usable self-serve
- **Time:** 20 min

People operations has some of the best low-risk uses and the highest-risk ones, side by side. The line between drafting a job posting and ranking candidates must be explicit.

## Outcomes

A participant can:

- Separate the uses that are safe to adopt from those that are not delegated.
- Handle candidate and employee information within the rules.
- Know when transparency toward candidates and employees is required.

## Topics

### Required

- Good uses: job posting drafts, interview question banks, onboarding plans, training material, policy drafts and plain-language versions, internal FAQs.
- Not delegated: screening, ranking or evaluating candidates and employees, compensation, discipline. These carry bias risk and legal obligations around automated decisions ([X-02](../../overlays/X-02-jurisdiction-and-policy-pack.md)) and need HR and legal review.
- Candidates and employees are identifiable people. Their information goes into approved tools only, limited to what is necessary, and anonymized for analysis.
- Tell candidates and employees about AI use where the law or policy requires it.

### Recommended

- Theme analysis of anonymized survey responses.
- Skills matrices and learning paths.
- Inclusive-language review.
- Consistent communication templates.
- Toolkits for managers.

### Optional

- Assistants built into the HR system.
- A policy question-and-answer assistant grounded in approved documents.
- Workforce planning scenarios.

## Checkpoint

> Which of these four would you do with an assistant today: rewrite a job posting, shortlist ten applications, summarize an engagement survey, draft a warning letter?

## Practice

Draft a job posting from a role description, then review it for biased wording and invented requirements.

## Re-verify before each delivery

- Legal obligations on automated decisions and on recruitment tools.
