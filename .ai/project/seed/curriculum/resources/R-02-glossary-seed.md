# R-02 · Glossary seed

The terms the lessons have to define, where each is introduced, and a proposed way to render it in Canadian French.

The French column is a set of **candidates, not decisions**. Each one has to be confirmed against the Grand dictionnaire terminologique and by a native reviewer before it is used (see the French language section of [R-01](R-01-further-reading.md)). Where the English term is what people actually say at work, the proposal is to keep it and give the French term once at first mention.

## Policy to confirm

1. A French term exists and is in common use: use it.
2. A French term exists and is rare: use the English term, and give the French term in parentheses at first mention.
3. No settled French term: keep English, in italics at first mention.
4. Product names, file names, commands and settings are never translated.
5. One term, one rendering, across every lesson and slide. The glossary is the reference.
