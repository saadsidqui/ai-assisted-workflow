# P-04 · Design and prototyping

- **Where it sits:** Product and experience track
- **Who:** Required: designers · Optional: PM / PO, analysts
- **Delivery:** Product and experience track, session 2; also usable self-serve
- **Time:** 25 min

Generating a hundred directions is now free, and they all look alike unless someone directs them. Design judgment moves from producing options to steering and choosing.

## Outcomes

A participant can:

- Brief an assistant for design work with constraints and named patterns to avoid.
- Critique generated work against explicit criteria.
- Use generated prototypes as learning tools without mistaking them for product.

## Topics

### Required

- Diverge with AI, converge yourself. The designer chooses and justifies.
- Brief with the audience, the purpose, the constraints, the design system and examples. Name what to avoid.
- Generic is the default result. Learn to recognize the common AI look and steer away from it with specific direction.
- Critique against explicit criteria (usability heuristics, accessibility guidelines), not "is this good".
- Accessibility is verified by people: contrast, keyboard, screen reader.
- Rights: generated imagery, fonts, likenesses, client brand rules.

### Recommended

- Prompt-to-prototype tools for clickable prototypes that are thrown away after the question is answered.
- Generation that respects the design system: tokens and components.
- Design-to-code handoff through connectors to the design tool.
- Review from screenshots.
- Traceability from research to design decision.

### Optional

- Image generation and editing workflows.
- Motion and video.
- Token pipelines.
- Logo and identity exploration.

## Checkpoint

> Two generated landing pages on screen. What makes them look machine-made? Name three specific patterns.

## Practice

Generate three directions for one screen, write the selection rationale in five lines, and list two accessibility checks that still need a person.

## Re-verify before each delivery

- Tool names and what current models default to visually.
