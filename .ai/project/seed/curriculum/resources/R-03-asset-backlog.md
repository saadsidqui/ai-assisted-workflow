# R-03 · Asset backlog

What has to be produced from this outline, in the order that makes each item usable by the next. Lessons and handouts are written in Canadian French; working files stay in English.

## Build order

1. **Glossary** ([R-02](R-02-glossary-seed.md)), because every lesson depends on its terms.
2. **Core lessons**, one file per core module, with speaker notes written out in full.
3. **Workshop packs** ([S-02](../session/S-02-workshop.md)), tested with the real tool and account type.
4. **Common-session slides**, built from the lessons, on demand.
5. **Track lessons**, starting with the track that has the most participants.
6. **Overlays**, with the local policy pack ([X-02](../overlays/X-02-jurisdiction-and-policy-pack.md)) reviewed by the organization's privacy lead.
7. **Track slides and labs**, on demand.

## Quality bar for every asset

- Organization-neutral: no real employer, client, team or private person appears. Public tools, vendors and sources are named only where a module calls for it.
- Every factual statement about a product is dated or sits in the snapshot.
- No invented statistics, quotations or testimonials.
- Checked for the patterns that mark text and visuals as machine-made, then reviewed by a person.
- Accessible: readable when projected, sufficient contrast, meaning never carried by colour alone.
- French reviewed by a native speaker against the glossary.
