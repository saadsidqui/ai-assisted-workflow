# 0016. The fictional engagement

- Date: 2026-11-10
- Status: accepted
- Supersedes: none

## Context

OD-11 asked for one invented client and project, settled in an interview with the author before the core lessons (build order, step 3). The engagement supplies the running example of the common session and the material of every workshop pack and track exercise (S-02, X-03, R-03). `deliverables.md` sets its definition of done.

## Decision

1. **Point of view.** The learners' own organization delivers the engagement and is never named. Only the client is invented.
2. **Client.** « Location Pavois », an event equipment rental company in Quebec, with no city or region named. It has about 60 employees, three branches (Nord, Sud, Est) and a two-person IT team. A web search on 2026-11-10 found no company of that name. The Quebec business registry was not checked.
3. **Project, phase 1.** An online reservation portal with real availability, reservation requests, customer accounts and a staff back office. Online payment and accounting integration are out of scope.
4. **Timeline.** Dates have a day and a month, no year. The discovery meeting brings out the deadline conflict between the general manager and the operations director. At kickoff, the general manager decides: a pilot in the Est branch from 15 May, with Nord and Sud in October.
5. **People.** Ten characters, four at the client and six in the delivering team, each with a first name and a role, without family names.
6. **Client rules on AI.** Only the delivering organization's approved assistant, under its organization account; no customer personal information, only an anonymized export; the team works in the client's repository and accounts.
7. **Placement.** Each exercise sits at one of the nine X-03 stages. Every exercise receives a prepared starting point, so that no track depends on another track's work. The brief fixes the facts of each starting point, and the exercise that uses it holds the full document.
8. **Shared story.** The story that P-02 produces and E-02 implements is written in full in the brief: a reservation request needs at least three days' notice before delivery or pick-up.
9. **Running example.** A one-page project summary for the client's branch staff, at kickoff. The first draft invents online payment; the second merges the pilot with the October rollout, and the verify step catches it.
10. **The brief.** `resources/fictional-engagement.md`, titled « Mandat fictif : Location Pavois », is a reference for presenters and authors. Participants see only the context paragraph that heads each pack, and the X-03 map.
11. **Approvals.** Each stage names who prepares its document, who approves it and the check performed.
12. **Terms.** The X-03 stage and artifact terms the brief needs are added to the glossary, checked in the GDT under decision 0014: « demande d’offres de services », « document de passation », « notes de mise à jour », « occasion d’affaires », « offre de services », « réunion de démarrage », « synthèse de découverte ».

## Consequences

- OD-11 is settled.
- Every lesson, pack and exercise takes its names, dates and figures from the brief. A new fact is added to the brief before it is used.
- The language of the sample code stays with OD-04 and OD-16. The E-03 labs use this project only if OD-16 chooses it.
- The S-02 discovery brief is settled now as « synthèse de découverte », earlier than planned.
