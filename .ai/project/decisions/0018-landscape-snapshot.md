# 0018. The landscape snapshot: owner, length and content

- Date: 2026-11-11
- Status: accepted
- Supersedes: none

## Context

OD-10 asked who owns the landscape snapshot (C-02) and how often it is refreshed, to be settled before the C-02 lesson. Writing the lesson raised three more points the card leaves open:

- its Time line reads "4 min in session, 15 min full", and decision 0017 does not say which figure is `minutes`. C-02 has no checkpoint row in S-01, because checkpoint 2 belongs to C-03;
- the map must show the organization's approved tools, but the lesson is organization-neutral;
- the map is easier to use with public products named as examples, and C-02 is the one module where product names and dated behaviour may appear.

The author approved all four points as proposed on 2026-11-11.

## Decision

1. **Owner (OD-10).** The presenter of record for each delivery owns the snapshot. They refresh it within the week before each delivery, and at least once a quarter. The lesson names the owner by role, never by person.
2. **Length.** The C-02 lesson has `minutes: 4`, with three slides and full speaker notes. The checkpoint, a show of hands, runs inside those four minutes and has its own row in the timing table. The 15-minute full version is the learner text, for reading alone and for the textbook.
3. **Approved tools.** The map slide has a column « Approuvé ici » that is left blank in the lesson. Before each delivery, the presenter fills it in from the organization's approved-tool list (X-02).
4. **Public examples.** The learner text names two or three public products per category as examples, not as a ranking. Each one is checked on a page of its vendor, and listed with the URL and the date of the check in the lesson's re-verify section. A product whose page could not be opened is left out.

## Consequences

- OD-10 is settled once this record is accepted.
- The re-verify section of C-02 is the refresh checklist: every product, URL and date is re-checked before each delivery.
- The 90-minute cut in S-01 ("the snapshot shrinks to a single slide") keeps the map slide only.
- The one-page snapshot reference in R-03 is built later, with the core handouts (build order, step 6), from this lesson.
