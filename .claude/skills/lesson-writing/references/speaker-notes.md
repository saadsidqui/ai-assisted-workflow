# Speaker notes

Speaker notes are the presenter's script. They are written in French and read in the notes pane while the slide shows. The presenter is new to teaching and speaks slowly.

## Form

- **Timing mark.** The block opens with one, for example `[3 min]`. The marks in a lesson add up to its timing table.
- **Full sentences, as said aloud.** The presenter speaks to the room as « vous ». Sentences are short, about 20 words at most, and one idea each.
- **Stage directions** go in square brackets, as imperatives addressed to the presenter: `[Pause]`, `[Passez à la diapositive suivante]`, `[Attendez deux ou trois réponses]`, `[Montrez l’exemple du doigt]`.
- **Budget:** about 110 words per planned minute of talk. Checkpoint and practice minutes belong to the room and are not counted. A three-minute explanation carries about 330 words, counting spoken sentences only.
- **Timing marks** may use a decimal comma, as in `[0,5 min]`.
- **Transition into each checkpoint:** written out word for word, in « ».
- **Late marker:** `[Si vous êtes en retard : passez directement à « titre de la diapositive ».]`, wherever a cut is allowed.
- **Typography:** French, as in `DESIGN.md` section 9.

## Content

- **Add to the slide.** The notes say what the slide does not: the reason, the example in words, the question to ask. Never read the slide aloud.
- **Say what to look at:** « Regardez la deuxième ligne de l’extrait. »
- **Write the question to the room as asked,** then the pause, then what to do with the answers.
- **Model the behaviour the program teaches.** Where the card asks for it, the presenter says « Je ne sais pas, je vais vérifier ».
- **No improvised facts.** Anything the presenter states as fact is in the lesson's learner text or its sources.

## Short illustration (off topic, for style only)

```text
[2 min] Voici un horaire d’autobus comme ceux qu’on trouve aux arrêts. [Pause] Cherchez le départ de 8 h 05. [Attendez que la salle le trouve.] Vous l’avez trouvé en lisant la colonne de gauche, puis la ligne. C’est la même lecture pour chaque tableau de ce module : d’abord la colonne, ensuite la ligne.
[Si vous êtes en retard : passez directement à « Lire un tableau en deux gestes ».]
```
