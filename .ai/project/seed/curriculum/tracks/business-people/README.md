# Business and people track

For management, HR, business development and everyone whose work is mainly documents, messages and decisions. The track starts with everyday writing and research, then covers the two functions where the line between safe and high-risk use matters most, and ends with what leadership has to put in place.

All eight core modules are required for everyone and come first: see the [curriculum index](../../README.md).

## Modules by role

Required means the role should not work with AI on this kind of task without it. Recommended means most people in the role benefit. Optional is for interest or a specific need.

## Suggested sessions

Each session is planned inside its booking with at least ten minutes left unallocated for questions and a slower pace. Module minutes come from the module cards and include each module's checkpoint.

### Session 1 · Writing, research and decisions

- Modules: [B-01](B-01-communications-and-documents.md), [B-02](B-02-research-and-decision-briefs.md) (40 min)
- Overlay: [X-01](../../overlays/X-01-working-in-a-client-environment.md) (10 min)
- Practice: Workshop: from rough notes to a client-ready message and an internal summary ([S-02](../../session/S-02-workshop.md)). (25 min)
- Planned: 75 min of a 90-minute booking, with 15 min unallocated

### Session 2 · People operations and business development

- Modules: [B-03](B-03-people-operations.md), [B-04](B-04-business-development.md) (40 min)
- Overlay: [X-03](../../overlays/X-03-end-to-end-engagement-map.md) (10 min)
- Practice: Exercise: a job posting review, or a compliance matrix from a fictional request for proposals. (15 min)
- Planned: 65 min of a 90-minute booking, with 25 min unallocated

### Leadership session · Leading adoption

- Modules: [B-05](B-05-leading-adoption.md) (25 min)
- Practice: Exercise: fill in the one-page adoption plan. (20 min)
- Planned: 45 min of a 60-minute booking, with 15 min unallocated
