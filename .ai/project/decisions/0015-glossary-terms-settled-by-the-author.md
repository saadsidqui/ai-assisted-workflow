# 0015. Glossary terms settled by the author

- Date: 2026-11-10
- Status: accepted
- Supersedes: none

## Context

Checking the R-02 card terms in the GDT left four terms that the policy of decision 0014 did not settle on its own: two pointed at a locked file, one at an old GDT record that has no noun form, and one at records from outside the OQLF.

## Decision

1. "Commit" and "push" stay in English under rule 4, as the names of the Git commands that learners type. The GDT term "archiver" is given once, at first mention.
2. "Accountability" is rendered "obligation de rendre compte". The GDT uses "responsabilité" for the obligation to act, a different concept, and advises against "imputabilité" in this sense.
3. "Handout" is rendered "document d’accompagnement", as in the GDT. The French half of `README.md` was changed to match, with the author's approval. "Fiche" now means only a module card or a GDT record.
4. "Engagement" is rendered "mandat" under rule 2. The GDT has no OQLF record for this sense, and its "mission" records come from an accounting dictionary that marks no preferred term.

## Consequences

- The glossary records each term with its rule and its sources.
- The E-02 and E-03 lessons use "commit" and "push" as nouns, and the verb forms the glossary gives.
- C-06 and the other cards that mention accountability say "obligation de rendre compte".
- The X-03 title stays « carte d’un mandat de bout en bout ».
