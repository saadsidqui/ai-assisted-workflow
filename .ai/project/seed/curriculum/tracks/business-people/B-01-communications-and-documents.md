# B-01 · Communications and documents

- **Where it sits:** Business and people track
- **Who:** Required: all business and people roles
- **Delivery:** Business and people track, session 1; also usable self-serve
- **Time:** 20 min

Most office work is reading and writing. This is where everyone outside engineering gets their first real gain, and where an unread message sent under your name does its damage.

## Outcomes

A participant can:

- Brief an assistant for a message or document and edit the result into their own voice.
- Summarize a long document and check the summary against it.
- Say what an office-suite assistant can see.

## Topics

### Required

- Draft, rewrite, shorten and adapt for an audience. Summarize long documents and threads that you provide.
- Brief with the audience, the purpose, the key points, the tone and the length. You supply the facts; the assistant supplies wording.
- Edit into your own voice, remove the filler, and check names, dates, figures and commitments.
- Assistants inside the office suite (mail, documents, spreadsheets, slides, meetings) can see your mailbox and your files. Know what that implies before asking.
- Nothing confidential in a tool that is not approved. Nothing sent unread.

### Recommended

- Templates and reusable briefs for recurring documents.
- Meeting preparation and follow-ups.
- From outline to slide deck: structure first, design second.
- Bilingual communications with consistent terminology.
- Turning notes into a procedure or a FAQ.

### Optional

- A custom assistant or project that carries the house style.
- Mail and calendar triage agents.
- Document automation.

## Checkpoint

> The draft says "as discussed, we will deliver by the 15th". Was that discussed? Who checks?

## Practice

The business workshop ([S-02](../../session/S-02-workshop.md)): from rough notes to a client-ready message and an internal summary.
