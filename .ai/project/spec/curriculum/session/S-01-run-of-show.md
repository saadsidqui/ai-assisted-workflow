# S-01 · Run of show for the common session

- **Who:** everyone, all roles together
- **Booking:** 120 minutes preferred, 90 minutes possible
- **Presenter profile assumed:** speaks slowly, new to teaching, takes a moderate number of questions

The session is planned shorter than its booking on purpose. A 120-minute booking carries 100 planned minutes and 20 minutes that belong to nobody. A slow pace and good questions use that margin; if they do not, the session ends early, which no audience has ever minded.

Only the *required* topics of each core module are taught here. Recommended and optional topics are reference material and track content.

## Capacity check

- Talking segments total 64 minutes in the long version. At a slow speaking pace that is roughly one slide every two minutes, so **about 30 content slides, 35 at most**. A deck longer than that will not fit, whatever the timing table says.
- Speaker notes are written out in full for a novice presenter, at roughly 110 words per minute of planned talk. A 12-minute segment is about 1,300 words of notes.
- One example runs through the whole session: the same fictional request appears in the demonstration, the checkpoints and the recap.

## Checkpoints

Each checkpoint is one question, a real pause, two or three answers from the room, and a one-sentence recap. The questions come from the module cards.

| Checkpoint | Question | What it confirms |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Name one thing the assistant cannot know about your project unless you give it. ([C-01](../core/C-01-mental-model-and-vocabulary.md)) | The mental model landed |
| 2 | Three tasks: green, yellow or red? ([C-03](../core/C-03-task-fit-and-limits.md)) | People can judge task fit |
| 3 | This request is weak. What is missing? ([C-04](../core/C-04-working-loop-and-prompting.md), [C-05](../core/C-05-context.md)) | Briefing and context |
| 4 | Five snippets: paste, do not paste, or it depends? ([C-07](../core/C-07-data-security-and-responsible-use.md)) | The data rule is usable |

Questions that go deeper than the session are written on a visible parking lot and answered afterwards in writing. Announce this in the welcome so that deferring a question is not a refusal.

## If the session runs late

Cut in this order, and say so out loud so the room knows it is deliberate:

1. Segment 6 becomes one sentence and a pointer to the track sessions.
2. The landscape snapshot ([C-02](../core/C-02-landscape-snapshot.md)) shrinks to a single slide.
3. The share-out at the end of the workshop is dropped.
4. The break shortens to five minutes.

Never cut checkpoint 1, checkpoint 4, or the verification step of the workshop. Those three carry the session's purpose.

## Before the session

- One week ahead: every participant can sign in to the approved tool. The workshop fails without this.
- The demonstration is recorded and the recording is one click away.
- A second person handles the chat, the parking lot and the clock.
- The workshop packs ([S-02](S-02-workshop.md)) are printed or shared, with pre-generated outputs ready in case the tool is unavailable.
- Optional pre-work, ten minutes: a short survey on current use and confidence. Repeat it after the session to measure the change.

## After the session

- Send the handout, the parking-lot answers and the track schedule within two days.
- Run the same survey again.
- Note which segments ran long and adjust this table. The plan is expected to change after the first delivery.
