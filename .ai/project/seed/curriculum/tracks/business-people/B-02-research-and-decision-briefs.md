# B-02 · Research and decision briefs

- **Where it sits:** Business and people track
- **Who:** Required: management, business development · Recommended: PM / PO, analysts, HR, others
- **Delivery:** Business and people track, session 1; also usable self-serve
- **Time:** 20 min

Research modes produce a cited report in minutes. The citations make it checkable, which is only useful if someone checks.

## Outcomes

A participant can:

- Commission a research report with a stated structure.
- Verify sources for date, relevance and whether they say what is claimed.
- Present a decision brief that separates fact, estimate and opinion.

## Topics

### Required

- Research modes with web access produce cited reports. Open the sources, check the dates, and confirm that each source says what the report claims.
- Ask for the structure you need: criteria, options, evidence, unknowns.
- Separate facts, estimates and opinions. Ask what would change the conclusion.
- For recent events and prices, require a live search. A model's memory has a cutoff date.
- The brief informs. A person decides.

### Recommended

- A decision brief template: context, options, trade-offs, recommendation, risks.
- Scenario exploration and a devil's-advocate pass.
- First-pass due diligence on vendors and partners from public sources.
- Summaries of regulations and policies, confirmed with a qualified person before anyone relies on them.

### Optional

- Scheduled, recurring monitoring.
- Internal knowledge assistants grounded in approved documents.

## Checkpoint

> The report cites eight sources. How many do you open before forwarding it, and which ones?

## Practice

Commission a short comparison of three fictional options, then verify two of its citations.
