# 0008. Specification, backlog and product names

- Date: 2026-11-10
- Status: accepted
- Supersedes: none

## Context

The planning input had two parts: a curriculum outline and a file of suggestions. Some of the suggestions are already in the cards. Some cards teach the mechanics of a named tool.

## Decision

1. **Specification:** the curriculum outline is the specification.
2. **Backlog:** `suggestions.md` is a backlog. An item binds only once a card reflects it or the author accepts it. Items not yet reflected are listed for accept or drop in `spec/open-decisions.md`.
3. **Product names:** public AI tools, vendors, standards and sources may be named where a card's topics require it. Version numbers, release numbers and dated product behaviour appear only in C-02, in "re-verify" lists and in dated resource lists.

## Consequences

- The engineering repo-setup lessons can teach one named tool as their worked example, without dating the rest of the program.
- Lessons do not take content from the backlog on their own initiative.
